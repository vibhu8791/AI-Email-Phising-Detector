1. Overview and Application Identity

The AI Phishing Email Detection System (“we”, “our”, or “the Service”), hosted at https://ai-email-phising-detector.onrender.com, is a cybersecurity monitoring application designed to help individuals and security analysts inspect incoming email communications for phishing attempts, spoofed domains, malicious links, and Business Email Compromise (BEC) patterns.

3. Information We Collect and Process

The application only collects data strictly necessary to provide phishing detection analysis:

  • Google Profile Identity: Your email address, profile display name, and unique Google account ID, collected during Google OAuth 2.0 authorization to bind your session to an isolated workspace.
  • Encrypted OAuth Tokens: Short-lived OAuth access tokens and offline refresh tokens provided by Google. These tokens are encrypted before storage in MongoDB using Fernet (AES-128 in CBC mode with SHA-256 HMAC). Tokens are never exposed in client-side code or browser storage.
  • Email Metadata: Message ID, Subject line, Sender address (From), Recipient address (To), and Date/Time timestamps for the recent emails you choose to synchronize (default: 10 messages).
  • Email Body & Link Content: Plaintext message snippets and HTML body markup extracted for algorithmic scanning. This content is analyzed to identify suspicious URLs, IP-based links, deceptive display names, urgent wire transfer language, and credential-harvesting keywords.
  • Custom Sandbox Submissions: Any custom email subjects, senders, and body texts manually submitted into the Sandbox Scanner for on-demand analysis.
  • Session Identifiers: Server-signed JSON Web Tokens (HS256 JWT) stored in your browser's local storage to maintain your authenticated session.

4. How Your Email Data is Processed

When you click Sync Gmail or submit text to the scanner, the following security pipeline runs:

  • Heuristic Security Analysis: Our engine scans extracted hyperlinks for raw IP addresses, homograph/punycode attacks, mismatched display vs destination domains, and suspicious top-level domains (.xyz, .top, .buzz, etc.). It scans body text for social engineering pressure tactics (“urgent wire transfer”, “immediate account suspension”).
  • Machine Learning Inference: A local Scikit-learn TF-IDF vectorizer and Logistic Regression classifier evaluates text features to compute a probabilistic phishing score (0% to 100%).
  • Composite Scoring: The system combines heuristic detections and ML probability into a Risk Score (0–100) and assigns an actionable status: SAFE, SUSPICIOUS, or PHISHING.
  • Real-Time Alerts: Detected threats are logged to your personal history and broadcast to your active dashboard via WebSocket.

5. Data Storage and Tenant Isolation

In production, scanned email records, computed risk scores, and account metadata are persisted in a MongoDB database.

  • Strict Tenant Partitioning: All stored records are indexed and queried strictly by the authenticated user_id. No user can view, search, or access another user's email records.
  • Cryptographic Verification: All API endpoints enforce server-side JWT Bearer token authentication. Unauthenticated requests are rejected with HTTP 401 Unauthorized.

6. Account Disconnection, Revocation & Data Deletion

You retain complete control over your accounts and data:

  • Disconnecting Accounts: You can disconnect any connected Gmail account at any time through the Settings or Header dropdown. Upon clicking Disconnect, the backend sends a formal revocation request to https://oauth2.googleapis.com/revoke and immediately purges the account credentials from the database.
  • Deleting Individual Records: Any scanned email record can be permanently deleted from the Email History view using the Delete button.
  • Full Data Purge: You can clear all scanned email records and threat history at any time using the Danger Zone > Reset Data action in Settings.
  • Google Account Revocation: You can independently revoke access at any time directly through your Google Account Security Settings.
[Configuration Notice • Retention Timeframe]: By default, scanned email records are retained until deleted or reset by the user. If you implement an automated rolling deletion policy (e.g., 30 or 60 days), update this section accordingly.
